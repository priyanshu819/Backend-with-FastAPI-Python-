Track Info is a secure and user-friendly Patient Management System designed to simplify hospital and clinic record management. It enables healthcare organizations to efficiently create, view, update, and delete patient records from a single platform. The system helps maintain organized patient information, reduces paperwork, improves data accessibility, and ensures that all records are stored securely. With Track Info, hospitals and healthcare staff can manage patient data quickly, accurately, and with confidence.
